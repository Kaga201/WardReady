# WardReady – VR Clinical Training (Alpha Prototype)

High-fidelity alpha prototype for HCI (ITS69004). WardReady is a web-based VR training app for nursing and medical students in Malaysia. One patient journey runs through five connected features:

1. **Skills Lab** – guided IV cannulation in VR with step order checking
2. **Virtual Ward** – bedside assessment of a deteriorating patient, live vital signs and SBAR escalation
3. **Code Blue CPR** – compressions with live rate and depth feedback, team roles and AED steps
4. **Triage Drill** – mass casualty scene, START tagging of 8 casualties
5. **Debrief & Logbook** – scores, timeline of mistakes, competency sign-off and instructor dashboard

Open `index.html` in any modern browser (phone, laptop or VR headset browser). Built with A-Frame (WebXR). All data is simulated; no real patient data is used.
