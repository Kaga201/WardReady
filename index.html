Code Blue

Module 3 · adult basic life support
🚨
Bed 12 is unresponsive and not breathing normallyCall for help, start compressions, and get the defibrillator (AED).
Targets for high-quality CPR
Compression rate100–120 per minute
Compression depth5–6 cm
Ratio without airway30 compressions : 2 breaths
PausesUnder 10 seconds
How to compress
With a headset: push down with both controllers on the virtual chest. On a phone: tap and hold the red pad in rhythm (a longer press = deeper compression), or place the phone on a pillow and push.
